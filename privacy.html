Introduction

PitanjaZaBaku is developed by Cosmic Production d.o.o., based in Split, Croatia. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Your family stories

The app saves your selected questions and the answers you write on your device. Cosmic Production does not receive your family stories. When you export or share a PDF, you choose where it goes in the iOS share sheet. The service or person you select can then receive the file.

Version 1.1.0 (build 4)

This version includes Google mobile advertising and consent software. Its App Store disclosure lists device identifiers, advertising data and product interactions for third-party advertising and analytics, and identifies these categories as used for tracking. You can manage the app’s tracking permission in iOS Settings. Google’s privacy policy describes Google’s processing.

Version 1.1.1 (build 6)

This version contains no advertising SDK and does not track you. It stores your book on your device. Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. Cosmic Production can view the reports Apple provides in App Store Connect. Apple’s privacy policy explains how Apple handles its services.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us.
